Returns & Refunds Policy

Last updated: April 2026

How it works here
Every DUBIS item is made to order, printed specifically for you the moment you place your order. That's why we can't accept returns for change of mind. Size is a different story: if the fit is off, message us on WhatsApp at +972-52-366-2526 within 30 days of delivery and we'll arrange a size exchange.

That said — we stand behind every item that leaves our production line. If something is wrong on our end, we'll fix it. No hassle, no drama.

What's Covered / What's Not

✅ We'll Replace or Refund

  • Size doesn't fit — size exchange via WhatsApp within 30 days
  • Manufacturing defect — cracked print, tear, stitching flaw
  • Wrong item — wrong size, color, or product sent
  • Shipping damage — arrived torn or crushed
  • Lost in transit — no tracking movement for 30+ days

❌ Not Covered

  • Change of mind or remorse
  • Slight color differences between screen and print
  • Damage from improper washing
  • Incorrect shipping address entered at checkout
  • Claims submitted after 30 days
About color differences: Printing is a chemical-digital process. Slight variations in shade between what you see on screen and the actual print are natural and not considered a defect.

How to File a Claim

  1. Email us at dubis.brand@gmail.com within 30 days of delivery
  2. Include your order number (from your confirmation email)
  3. Attach clear photos of the defect or wrong item — required
  4. Let us know your preference: reprint or refund

What Happens Next

We'll review your claim within 2 business days. If approved:

  • Reprint — we'll produce and ship a new item ASAP, at no cost to you
  • Refund — returned to your original payment method (PayPal) within 5–10 business days

If a reprint isn't feasible or you can't wait — we'll issue a refund instead.

Size Exchanges

Wrong size? It happens. Message us on WhatsApp at +972-52-366-2526 (or by email) within 30 days of delivery with your order number and the size you need, and we'll arrange the exchange. Some styles are held in local stock in Israel, so exchanges there can be extra fast.
