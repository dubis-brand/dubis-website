Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

In short: we collect only what it takes to fulfill your order and run the store. We never sell personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers an order literally cannot happen without (payment, printing, shipping) and with the standard measurement tools listed in full below.

This policy covers www.dubis.net ("the Site"), operated by the DUBIS brand ("we", "us"). It describes what actually happens, not boilerplate.

Privacy contact: dubis.brand@gmail.com or WhatsApp +972-52-366-2526.

1. What We Collect

When you place an order

  • Full name, shipping address, phone number, email address
  • The items you ordered, the order total, and a transaction ID
  • Your card details never reach us. Payment is handled entirely by PayPal (including regular card payments made without a PayPal account)

When you sign up for updates or leave your email

  • The email address you provided. If you leave an email while items are in your cart, we may send a single reminder with a cart-restore link

When you browse

  • Basic usage data: pages and products viewed, a random visit identifier
  • Your country, derived from your IP address, so we can show the right language and shipping options
  • How you arrived at the site (for example a campaign or social network), for marketing measurement

3. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell personal information. Period. Data goes only to the service providers the store runs on, and each one gets only what it needs:

  • Gelato (printing and shipping): name, address, phone, and item details, to produce and deliver your order
  • PayPal (payment): processes the entire payment, under PayPal's own privacy policy
  • Supabase (database) and Vercel (hosting): the infrastructure the site runs on
  • Resend (email delivery): order confirmations and updates
  • Meta, Microsoft, Google: the measurement tools listed in section 2

Beyond that, we disclose data only if required by law.

4. Email

  • Transactional emails (order confirmation, shipping updates) go to every customer
  • Marketing email goes only to subscribers, with an unsubscribe option in every message. Replying "unsubscribe" works too

5. Security

The site is served over encrypted HTTPS only. Database access is restricted and secured. Payment details, as noted, are never stored by us at all.

6. Data Retention

Order data is kept as long as needed for service, accounting, and legal requirements. Analytics data follows the retention policies of the tools in section 2. A subscribed email is kept until you ask to be removed.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Email dubis.brand@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days, usually much sooner.

8. California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

  • Categories we collect: identifiers (name, email, address, phone, IP), commercial information (orders), and internet activity (pages viewed, referral source)
  • We do not sell personal information. We use advertising measurement tools (Meta Pixel) that may be considered "sharing" under California law; you can opt out of that by blocking the pixel as described in section 2 or by contacting us
  • Your rights: to know what we hold, to request deletion, to opt out of sharing, and to not be discriminated against for exercising any of these. Submit requests to dubis.brand@gmail.com

9. Children

The Site is intended for purchasers aged 18 and up. We do not knowingly collect data about minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy we will change the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be flagged prominently on the site.
