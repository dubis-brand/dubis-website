How DUBIS started

A year ago my wife and I wandered into a clothing store. Just browsing. I looked at the walls — young, chiseled models in every corner — and said to her: why is there no brand for regular guys? Forty-plus, a bit of a belly, a little less hair, with nothing left to prove. I tossed out a line: someone should build that brand. Call it DUBIS. We laughed, walked out, and I filed it away.

Six months later I started building it — but differently. I have lived and breathed AI for years, and this time I asked: could a real, commercial business run entirely on AI agents, and still feel human? So, at night after work, DUBIS began.

I built the site, the catalog, the link to the print house that ships straight to your door. Then I built a team of agents — one writes, one designs, one runs supply, one sales, one security. Above them, a Boss who runs them all, with a weekly team meeting.

I know how it sounds. Wild. But it is a living business — real products, real customers. Still in beta. Still improving.
