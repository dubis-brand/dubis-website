# Brand Content Guidelines — DUBIS

## Brand Identity
- **Tone:** Self-aware humor, body-positive, relatable, comfortable-in-your-own-skin
- **Target audience:** Plus-size / body-positive, comfort-first fashion, ages 25-45
- **Brand colors:** Dark navy, gold/tan, black, white, charcoal
- **Logo:** Small "DUBIS™" text front chest (left side), DUBIS bear on some items

## Content Rules
- Captions: Hebrew ONLY for Hebrew audience. English for English audience. Never mix.
- NEVER use generic stock descriptions — always tie to specific DUBIS product & slogan
- Product images should show the ACTUAL slogan clearly
- Always use the correct typography hierarchy (see brand-typography.md)

## Reel / Talking Photo Requirements
- ONE person, face visible, facing camera
- Person wearing the RELEVANT product (hoodie for hoodie content, t-shirt for t-shirt)
- No sunglasses covering face
- Good lighting, clear face

## Hashtag Strategy
- Hebrew: #דוביס #קפוצון #אופנהפלאססייז #גודלזהלאמגבלה
- English: #DUBIS #BodyPositive #PlusSizeFashion #ComfortFirst
